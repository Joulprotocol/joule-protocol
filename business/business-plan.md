# JOULE Business Plan — Community-Driven Protocol

## Model

JOULE is a community-owned protocol. No company, no founder fee, no VC.
Revenue comes from participation, not extraction.

## Cost Structure

### Development: 0 EUR
- Open source, community-developed
- AI-assisted development tooling

### Launch: ~5,000 EUR
- Security audit / bug bounty: ~2,000 EUR
- Initial DEX liquidity: ~2,000 EUR  
- Domain + hosting: ~200 EUR
- Community setup: ~500 EUR

### Operation: ~750 EUR/month
- VPS backup node: ~50 EUR
- Community management: volunteer
- Marketing: organic + community
- Domain: ~15 EUR/month

## Go-to-Market

1. GPU mining community (fair launch, no premine)
2. Energy producers (3x PoE rewards = free extra revenue)
3. AI/IoT developers (SDK, agent wallets)
4. Carbon credit buyers (EU compliance)

## Competitive Advantage

- Only PoW chain with real energy backing
- 1 JOL = 1 kWh floor price
- Fair launch, no insider advantage
- Full EVM compatibility
- Machine economy native (payment channels, streaming, agent wallets)
