# JOULE Regulatory Strategy

## Classification

JOULE is a fair-launch, community-mined cryptocurrency.
No token sale, no ICO, no public offering.

### MiCA Applicability
- **No public offering** → MiCA whitepaper not mandatory
- **Mining rewards** → not a securities offering (Howey test: no "efforts of others")
- **DEX listing** → permissionless, no entity required
- **Utility function** → gas payments, energy redemption, governance

### Comparable Precedent
- Bitcoin: no identified issuer, no whitepaper obligation, globally traded
- JOULE follows the same model

## Tax Considerations (General)

Mining income is generally taxable at the market value at the time of receipt.
HODLing is not taxable until disposal in most EU jurisdictions.
Each participant is responsible for their own tax compliance.
